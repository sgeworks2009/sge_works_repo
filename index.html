Hard Chrome Plating & Precision Grinding in Pune

Shree Gurudatta Engineering Works (SGE Works), established in 2009, provides industrial hard chrome plating and surface engineering job work from its facility in MIDC Bhosari, Pune.

Promoted and managed by Director Mr. Shivaji Mahajan and CEO Mr. D. S. Mahajan, our team brings hands-on industrial expertise to the precision coating and finishing of cylinders, shafts, rollers, and machinery components.

We operate three dedicated plating tanks accommodating component lengths up to 3000 mm in Tank 02 and diameters up to Ø400 mm, alongside cylindrical grinding machinery in our Bhosari workshop. Supported by an electric crane system handling components up to 2 tonnes, we deliver high-hardness (62+ HRC), wear-resistant surfaces finished to exact drawing tolerances.

HEAVY WEAR PROTECTION

Hard Chrome Plating

We apply industrial hard chrome plating up to 600 microns (0.6 mm) on cylinders, hydraulic rods, pistons, spindles, rollers, shafts, and mandrels. Designed to improve surface hardness, wear resistance, and component service life.

MAX CHROME THICKNESS Up to 600 Microns (0.6 mm)

PRIMARY BENEFIT Extremely hard surface (62+ HRC) resisting friction & abrasion

THIN-LAYER COATING

Flash Chrome Plating

A thin, uniform chrome coating (15 to 30 microns) for pipes, stamping dies, small pins, fixtures, and machine base plates. Protects against corrosion and gives smooth part release without altering tight machined tolerances.

COATING THICKNESS 15 to 30 Microns
COMMON COMPONENTS Pipes, small pins, stamping dies, molding tooling, machine plates
PRIMARY BENEFIT Corrosion protection and smooth part release for engineering components
CIRCULAR DIES & TOOLING · TAP FOR GALLERY 15–30 μm

QUOTE CHECKLIST

Information to Provide for a Quotation

Providing these details helps our team review your requirements and respond with a relevant quotation:

  • Component Name & Dimensions: Component name, outside diameter (Ø in mm), and length (in mm).
  • Quantity & Material: Number of parts and base metal or material grade (if known).
  • Coating Type & Thickness: Hard chrome (up to 600 μm), flash chrome (15–30 μm), or satin chrome (10–25 μm).
  • Existing Surface Condition: New component manufacturing or wear reconditioning/stripping.
  • Grinding & Finishing: Pre-grind, finish grind, or polishing requirements, along with component drawings or photographs.
