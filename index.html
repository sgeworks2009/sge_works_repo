Hard Chrome Plating & Precision Grinding in Pune

Shree Gurudatta Engineering Works (SGE Works) is an industrial hard chrome plating and precision cylindrical grinding workshop located in MIDC Bhosari, Pune.

Started in 2009 by Director Mr. Shivaji Mahajan and managed by CEO Mr. D. S. Mahajan, our team brings over 15 years of hands-on technical experience in hard chrome plating, cylindrical grinding, and precision component reconditioning.

We operate our own primary 3,000-litre chemical plating tank and secondary 2,000-litre tank alongside heavy cylindrical grinding machinery handling lengths up to 3000 mm in a fully integrated Bhosari plant. We service OEM production runs as well as heavy wear reclamation—such as hydraulic cylinder rods, calender rollers, drive shafts, and tooling dies—delivering high-hardness (62+ HRC / 800–1000 HV), corrosion-resistant surfaces to exact drawing tolerances.

Our Plating & Grinding Services

We provide hard chrome plating, flash chroming, cylindrical grinding, buffing, and chrome removal for new machine parts and worn-out components.

HEAVY WEAR PROTECTION

Hard Chrome Plating

We apply industrial hard chrome plating up to 600 microns (0.6 mm) on cylinders, hydraulic rods, pistons, spindles, rollers, shafts, and mandrels. This creates a hard, durable surface that stops wear, reduces friction, and prevents rusting.

MAX CHROME THICKNESS Up to 600 Microns (0.6 mm)
COMMON COMPONENTS Hydraulic cylinder rods, pistons, drive shafts, rollers, spindles
PRIMARY BENEFIT Extremely hard surface (62+ HRC / 800–1000 HV) resisting heavy friction & abrasion
HYDRAULIC RODS · TAP FOR GALLERY 62+ HRC
THIN-LAYER COATING

Flash Chrome Plating

A thin, uniform chrome coating (15 to 30 microns) for pipes, stamping dies, small pins, fixtures, and machine base plates. Protects against corrosion and gives smooth part release without altering tight machined tolerances.

COATING THICKNESS 15 to 30 Microns
COMMON COMPONENTS Pipes, small pins, stamping dies, molding tooling, machine plates
PRIMARY BENEFIT Rust protection and easy part release without altering dimensions
PLATES, PIPES & PINS · TAP FOR GALLERY 15–30 μm
MATTE ANTI-GLARE FINISH

Satin Chrome Plating

A non-glare, fine-grained satin/pearl chrome finish engineered for precision molding dies, optical equipment, instrument dials, and automotive switchgear. Provides superior corrosion and wear resistance without the blinding light reflections of mirror chrome, eliminating operator eyestrain under intense workshop illumination.

COATING THICKNESS 10 to 25 Microns
COMMON COMPONENTS Plastic injection molds, automotive dials, stamping dies, measuring instruments
PRIMARY BENEFIT Anti-glare surface ergonomics with heavy corrosion & scratch resistance
SATIN MATTE CHROME · TAP FOR GALLERY 10–25 μm
