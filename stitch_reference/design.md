# SGE Works — Design System & Website Direction
## 01. Project
Design and prototype a premium single-page website for:
**Shree Gurudatta Engineering Works (SGE Works)**
Business:
Hard Chrome Plating & Surface Engineering
Location:
Bhosari, Pune, Maharashtra, India
Established:
2009
The website must feel like a serious industrial engineering company, not a generic manufacturing website, SaaS website, corporate template, or brochure converted into a webpage.
The visual quality should feel closer to a premium industrial design studio / engineering publication.
---
# 02. Core Design Direction
## Industrial Editorial
The visual language combines:
- Industrial photography
- Precision engineering
- Strong typography
- Technical information
- Fine borders
- Structured grids
- Asymmetrical compositions
- Chrome and metallic visual details
- Deep industrial navy
- Controlled orange accents
- Subtle purposeful motion
The design should communicate:
**Precision. Engineering. Infrastructure. Experience. Reliability.**
The website should make the visitor feel:
> These people handle serious industrial components and understand surface engineering.
Do not make the site look like an AI-generated corporate website.
---
# 03. Single-Page Architecture
This is a SINGLE-PAGE website.
Do not create separate pages for About, Services, Capabilities, Industries or Contact.
The entire experience exists on one page with smooth scrolling.
Primary structure:
1. Hero
2. About
3. Services
4. Capabilities
5. Industries
6. Contact
7. Footer
Additional content such as components, facility, quality and client references should be incorporated into these major sections rather than becoming additional navigation items.
---
# 04. Navigation
Keep the navigation compact.
Desktop navigation:
**SGE WORKS**
About  
Services ▾  
Capabilities  
Industries  
Contact  
**Request a Quote →**
The navigation should remain sticky.
Initially, it may sit over the hero image with a transparent/dark treatment.
After scrolling, transition smoothly into a solid dark/navy background.
Use a thin 1px border.
Do not use a large navigation bar.
Do not create a mega-menu.
---
# 05. Services Dropdown
When the cursor hovers over **Services**, show a small, elegant dropdown.
Items:
- Hard Chrome Plating
- Flash Chrome Plating
- Precision Grinding
- Buffing & Polishing
- De-plating / Chrome Removal
Each item should scroll directly to its corresponding service subsection.
The dropdown should be:
- Compact
- Dark
- Technical
- Minimal
- Clearly readable
- Slightly animated
Do not create a large mega-menu.
On mobile, Services becomes a tap-based expandable menu.
---
# 06. Hero
The hero is the most important visual component.
It must occupy the entire viewport below the navigation.
Use:
**100vw × viewport height minus navigation**
The hero must contain a large authentic industrial photograph.
Potential imagery:
- Hard chrome plated hydraulic rods
- Chrome-plated shafts
- Industrial rollers
- Plating tanks
- Grinding operations
- Industrial components
- SGE Works facility
## Hero composition
Text sits directly over the image.
Do NOT place the text inside:
- Cards
- Glass panels
- Floating boxes
- Opaque containers
The image is the primary visual element.
## Hero gradient
Apply a dark gradient originating from the bottom-right corner.
The gradient should become strongest behind the text and fade naturally into the image.
The purpose is:
1. Improve text readability
2. Create visual depth
3. Preserve the photograph
4. Integrate the typography with the image
Do not apply a flat black overlay across the entire image.
The gradient should adapt to the image composition.
## Hero content
Use:
Small technical label:
**EST. 2009 · BHOSARI, PUNE**
Primary H1:
**Industrial Hard Chrome Plating & Surface Engineering**
Supporting copy should be concise and factual.
Primary CTA:
**Request a Quote →**
Secondary CTA:
**Explore Services ↓**
The H1 should be large, confident and highly readable.
Do not center everything by default.
Position the text according to the image composition, preferably toward the lower-left or lower-right.
---
# 07. Hero Image Language
The hero establishes the visual language for the entire website.
Image first.
Typography second.
Interface third.
Use photography with:
- Strong contrast
- Visible material texture
- Metal surfaces
- Machinery
- Industrial scale
- Real working environments
Avoid:
- Generic stock manufacturing imagery
- Corporate handshake photographs
- Generic factory workers posing for camera
- Artificial 3D renders
- Futuristic CGI
- Generic blue technology imagery
Real client photography should be preferred.

# 19. Color System
The existing brand direction is based around:
### Industrial Navy
Primary dark colour.
Use for:
- Navigation
- Dark sections
- Footer
- Backgrounds
- Technical surfaces
### Chrome / Metallic Silver
Introduce as an important secondary visual layer.
Use for:
- Fine borders
- Technical numbers
- Small labels
- Dividers
- Selected typography
- Subtle UI details
The metallic quality should primarily come from real chrome photography and restrained silver tones.
### Orange
Use as the primary accent.
Use for:
- CTA
- Hover state
- Small highlights
- Important interaction elements
Orange should remain controlled.
### White / Warm Light Neutral
Use for:
- Light backgrounds
- Main text on dark surfaces
- Supporting content
## Avoid
- Purple
- Pink
- Neon colours
- Electric blue
- Green
- Excessive gradients
- Fake metallic gradients
- Glowing chrome effects
The site should feel metallic through photography, typography and restraint, not through visual gimmicks.
---
# 20. Typography
Typography must feel deliberate and premium.
Do NOT default to generic AI-generated website typography.
Avoid automatically using:
- Inter
- Roboto
- Open Sans
- Poppins
- Montserrat
- Arial
Use a strong display typeface paired with a highly readable supporting typeface.
Typography hierarchy should be obvious:
- Large editorial H1
- Strong section headings
- Technical labels
- Large capability numbers
- Compact body copy
- Small uppercase metadata
Use typography as a major design element.
---
# 21. Grid & Layout
Use a 12-column desktop grid.
Prefer:
- Asymmetrical compositions
- Edge-aligned content
- Variable-width text blocks
- Full-width images
- Large editorial crops
- Horizontal compositions
- Intentional overlaps
- Tight but comfortable spacing
Avoid excessive empty margins at the left and right edges.
Do not constrain every section to a small centered container.
Use the viewport.
Allow images to break out of the main content grid.
---
# 22. Borders
Fine 1px borders are a fundamental part of the visual system.
Use them for:
- Navigation
- Technical specifications
- Service divisions
- Industry list separators
- Capability sections
- Footer
- Small UI controls
Borders should be subtle.
Avoid thick decorative borders.
---
# 23. Image Treatment
Photography is one of the primary design elements.
Use:
- Full-bleed photographs
- Large editorial crops
- Horizontal image strips
- Split compositions
- Edge-to-edge imagery
- Close-up metal textures
Do not put every image inside a rounded card.
Avoid excessive border radius.
Industrial photography should feel physical and tactile.
---
# 24. Sliders
Use sliders only where they add genuine visual value.
Recommended:
1. Technical capability marquee
2. Components slider
3. Client logo rail
4. Optional facility image gallery
Movement should be:
- Slow
- Controlled
- Subtle
- Continuous where appropriate
The site must remain fully usable without animation.
Important:
Never hide essential SEO content exclusively inside a slider.
---
# 25. Motion
Motion should feel engineered rather than decorative.
Use:
- Smooth anchor scrolling
- Subtle section reveals
- Controlled image movement
- Horizontal marquee
- Small hover transitions
- Navigation transitions
- Subtle image parallax where appropriate
Avoid:
- Excessive bouncing
- Large spinning animations
- 3D objects
- Glowing effects
- Particle effects
- Excessive blur
- Dramatic transitions
The site should remain visually strong with reduced motion enabled.
---
# 26. SEO Requirements
SEO is a core design requirement, not a post-design activity.
The website must use:
- One clear H1
- Logical H2/H3 hierarchy
- Semantic HTML
- Crawlable text
- Descriptive page title
- Meta description
- Canonical URL
- Open Graph metadata
- Descriptive image filenames
- Natural image alt text
- Internal anchor links
- Optimized images
- Fast loading
- Mobile-friendly layouts
- Structured data
Important business information must NEVER exist only inside images.
Services, capabilities, components, industries, location and company information must exist as actual HTML content.
---
# 27. Local SEO
The website must clearly establish:
**Shree Gurudatta Engineering Works**
as a:
**Hard Chrome Plating & Surface Engineering company in Bhosari, Pune, Maharashtra.**
Naturally incorporate relevant local/service language such as:
- Hard chrome plating in Pune
- Hard chrome plating in Bhosari
- Industrial chrome plating
- Surface engineering
- Flash chrome plating
- Precision grinding
- Chrome removal
- Hydraulic cylinder rod plating
- Piston rod plating
- Shaft and spindle plating
Do not keyword-stuff.
The content must read naturally for an industrial buyer.
---
# 28. GEO / Generative Search Optimization
Design the content so search engines and AI systems can clearly answer:
### Who is SGE Works?
Shree Gurudatta Engineering Works.
### What does it do?
Hard Chrome Plating & Surface Engineering.
### Where is it located?
Bhosari, Pune, Maharashtra.
### When was it established?
2009.
### What services does it provide?
Five clearly named services.
### What components does it handle?
Hydraulic rods, piston rods, shafts, spindles, rollers, mandrels and other industrial components.
### What industries does it serve?
Engineering, hydraulic, automotive, tool & die, process equipment and related industries.
### What are its capabilities?
600 μm coating, Ø6–400 mm diameter, 50–3000 mm job length and up to 2,000 L chemical capacity.
### How can customers contact it?
Phone, WhatsApp, email and physical location.
This information must be available as crawlable HTML.
Do not create artificial "AI SEO" copy.
Clarity and factual consistency are more important.
---
# 29. Structured Data
Implement appropriate JSON-LD structured data.
Use an appropriate LocalBusiness schema structure containing verified:
- Business name
- Address
- Telephone
- Email
- URL
- Logo
- Images
- Geographic information where appropriate
- Opening hours where confirmed
- SameAs links where appropriate
Do not publish unverified business information.
The official Name, Address and Phone must be confirmed before launch.
---
# 30. Image SEO & Performance
Images should be optimized using:
- WebP or AVIF where appropriate
- Responsive image sizes
- `srcset`
- Compression
- Descriptive filenames
- Natural alt text
- Lazy loading below the fold
The primary hero image should not be unnecessarily lazy-loaded.
Example filename:
`hard-chrome-plating-hydraulic-cylinder-rods-pune.webp`
Not:
`IMG_48291.webp`
Do not keyword-stuff alt text.
---
# 31. Performance
The site is image-heavy, so performance must be considered from the beginning.
Prioritize:
- Server-rendered/static HTML
- Optimized images
- Minimal JavaScript
- Responsive images
- Lazy loading
- Efficient fonts
- Limited animation
- Good Core Web Vitals
- Fast initial page load
The visual design must not sacrifice performance.
---
# 32. Mobile
Mobile should preserve the same design language.
Do not simply stack the desktop cards.
Maintain:
- Large imagery
- Strong typography
- Technical specification strips
- Horizontal touch sliders
- Fine borders
- Dark/navy surfaces
- Chrome/silver accents
Hero remains full-screen with the text positioned over the image and the gradient adjusted for readability.
Navigation becomes compact.
Services becomes expandable.
Floating WhatsApp and Call buttons remain visible.
---
# 33. What NOT To Do
Do not use:
- Generic SaaS layouts
- Generic corporate templates
- Excessive rounded cards
- Glassmorphism
- Purple/neon gradients
- Abstract blobs
- 3D illustrations
- Fake chrome effects
- Huge centered hero copy
- Floating UI cards over the hero
- Repetitive three-card grids
- Excessive whitespace
- Generic stock photography
- Decorative animation
- AI-looking typography
- Information hidden only inside images
- SEO keyword stuffing
Do not make the website look like a brochure pasted into a web page.
---
# 34. Final Design Test
Before approving the design, ask:
1. Does it immediately communicate Hard Chrome Plating & Surface Engineering?
2. Does it look like an established industrial company?
3. Does the photography feel authentic?
4. Does the chrome/metallic treatment feel physical rather than artificial?
5. Is the navigation simple?
6. Is Services easy to explore?
7. Can a visitor quickly understand the technical capabilities?
8. Can a visitor identify the industries and components served?
9. Is the contact information immediately accessible?
10. Are WhatsApp and Call always available?
11. Does the page feel custom-designed rather than templated?
12. Are the sections visually varied?
13. Does the website use the viewport effectively?
14. Are the important business facts available as HTML?
15. Does the content make sense to both search engines and generative search systems?
16. Does it still look good with animation disabled?
17. Does it feel like **SGE Works**, rather than a generic engineering company?
---
# 35. Design North Star
The final website should communicate:
> **These people know industrial surface engineering, have the infrastructure to handle serious components, and present themselves with precision and professionalism.**
The visual language should feel:
**Industrial. Precise. Metallic. Editorial. Confident.**
Not flashy.
Not corporate-template-like.
Not AI-generated.
Not a brochure.
A serious industrial engineering website with the visual quality of a modern design studio.
