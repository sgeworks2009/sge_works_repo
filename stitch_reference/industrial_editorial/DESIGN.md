## Brand & Style

This design system expresses heavy industrial precision, metallurgical mastery, and technical authority. Rooted in hard chrome plating and heavy surface engineering, the visual philosophy rejects generic software/SaaS tropes (floating pill cards, vibrant gradients, frosted glass, rounded bubbles) in favor of an **Industrial Editorial** aesthetic.

The visual tone is severe, deliberate, and architectural:
- **Atmosphere**: Authentic workshop reality, molten heat, machined steel, and calibrated chemical baths rather than sterile corporate stock photography.
- **Audience**: Tier-1 automotive manufacturers, heavy machinery OEMs, aerospace procurement leads, and precision engineering managers seeking zero-tolerance quality and verifiable technical metrics.
- **Emotional Response**: Raw physical permanence, metallurgical certainty, uncompromising precision, and unembellished shop-floor credibility.
- **Design Idiom**: Sharp structural hairlines (1px), asymmetrical grid alignments, high-density technical metric strips, and typography layered directly against authentic workshop imagery.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-contrast industrial discipline:
- **Base Canvas (`#0B1320`, `#0E1726`)**: Deep industrial navy replaces sterile blacks, echoing darkened plating vats and heavy cast metal. It serves as the primary canvas across navigation, hero backing, and module frames.
- **Chrome & Silver Secondary (`#94A3B8`, `#CBD5E1`, `#E2E8F0`)**: Restrained metallic tones define structural hairline dividers, mono readouts, and engineering indices. They provide cold, calibrated contrast without resort to faux gradient skeuomorphism.
- **Furnace Orange Accent (`#EA580C`, `#F97316`)**: Reserved strictly for functional conversions, active states, and critical mechanical alerts. It channels safety signage, molten pre-heat temperatures, and industrial machinery markers. It is never applied decoratively across broad surfaces.
- **Hairlines (`#1E293B` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`)**: Razor-thin dividers partition content directly on the surface grid, keeping interfaces planar, grounded, and free of floating card artifacts.

## Typography

The typography pairings enforce deliberate contrast between heavy mechanical geometry and technical specification indexing:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an unapologetic, engineered architectural presence. Tightly kerned at display scales to mimic stamped metal plates and high-impact industrial signage.
- **Body (`Work Sans`)**: Grounded, utilitarian, and exceptionally legible in dense industrial narratives. It avoids stylistic eccentricities, ensuring maximum clarity for engineering copy.
- **Labels & Metrics (`Space Mono`)**: Reserved for indices (`01 / ABOUT`), technical capabilities (`600 μm`, `Ø6–400 mm`), dimensional tolerances, and metadata tags (`EST. 2009 · BHOSARI, PUNE`). It emphasizes mathematical calibration, tabular alignment, and metallurgical data integrity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an asymmetrical 12-column technical grid that rejects standard centralized SaaS containers. Content expands edge-to-edge with generous horizontal canvas utilization and zero-radius hairline framing.

- **Grid Mechanics**: A 12-column desktop grid with 1.5rem gutters collapsing to 4 columns on mobile (1rem gutter).
- **Asymmetrical Alternation**: Sections cycle rhythmically between full-bleed raw workshop photography, staggered data columns, and raw text blocks aligned directly across grid lines.
- **Technical Specification Strips**: Continuous horizontal metric ribbons spanning the full viewport width, delimited by vertical 1px hairline dividers with calibrated mono values.
- **Hero Staging**: Hero units span `100vw × calc(100vh - 4.5rem)` with directional vignette overlays concentrated in the lower corner to hold large display type directly over imagery without opaque card wrappers.
- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 768px`), Tablet (`768px - 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through structural layering, optical contrast, and crisp planar boundaries rather than ambient drop shadows or blurred glass panes.

- **Zero Drop Shadows**: All elements sit planar on the canvas. No soft elevation shadows (`box-shadow`) are permitted on cards, buttons, or modules.
- **Planar Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base Canvas`: Deep Navy (`#0B1320`) background.
  - `Active Workplane`: Elevated surfaces (`#162032`) inset cleanly with 1px border lines (`#1E293B`).
  - `Navigation Layer`: Pinned to the top viewport edge; transparent over full-bleed hero visuals, transitioning to solid `#0B1320` with a 1px bottom border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) on scroll.
- **Photographic Depth**: Directional contrast gradients (fade from `#0B1320` at bottom-right up into transparent imagery) yield clear text legibility while exposing real machining finishes.
- **Floating Controls**: The persistent communication dock (WhatsApp / Call) is pinned to the bottom-right corner using a solid, bordered container without blurry diffusion or glow.

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Sharp (`0`)** shape language:
- Every interactive element, container, input field, tag, and modal has a `0px` border radius (`rounded-none`).
- Rounded corners, circular badges, and pill buttons are explicitly prohibited to reflect machined steel plate, shearing equipment, and laser-cut metal fabrication.
- Structural borders are universally 1px solid (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` or `#1E293B`).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action (CTA)**: Rectangular, sharp (`0px`), background `#EA580C`, high-contrast white text (`Work Sans` Bold / Semibold), uppercase with directional glyph (`REQUEST A QUOTE →`). Hover transition: shifts smoothly to `#F97316`. No drop shadows.
- **Secondary Action**: Sharp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), transparent background, silver-white text. Hover fills background with `rgba(226, 216, 240, 0.08)`.
- **Anchor Link**: Understated inline link with `Space Mono` text accompanied by precise arrow characters (`Explore Services ↓`, `01 — Technical Specs →`).

### Technical Specification Strips (Marquee / Metric Ribbon)
- Full-bleed band bound by top and bottom 1px hairlines.
- Segmented into equidistant cells by vertical 1px rules.
- Each cell contains an uppercase `Space Mono` micro-label above a prominent `metric-display` readout (`600 μm`, `Ø6–400 mm`, `2,000 L`).

### Industry & Service Index Rows
- Replaces standard cards with an editorial numbered index list.
- Rows separated by 1px hairlines (`#1E293B`).
- Structure: Index number (`01`, `02`) in `Space Mono` furnace orange, followed by a bold section title in `Space Grotesk`, a concise technical description in `Work Sans`, and a right-aligned indicator arrow (`→`).
- Hover state: Background subtly shifts from `#0B1320` to `#162032` with an immediate highlight on the indicator arrow.

### Forms & Input Fields
- Dark planar inputs with solid `#0E1726` surface and 1px `#1E293B` perimeter.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#EA580C` with zero outer glow.
- Labels rendered in uppercase `Space Mono` above the input.
- Monospaced helper text and metric dimension indicators directly inside inputs (e.g., `Length (mm)`).

### Persistent Emergency / Quick Connect Dock
- Bottom-right anchored dual-action console for high-priority technical inquiries (WhatsApp & Direct Shop Floor Call).
- Rigid rectangular enclosure with a 1px border.
- Crisp icons accompanied by monospaced telephone/chat indicators for rapid conversion.
